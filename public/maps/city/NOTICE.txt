City arena maps

rome, barcelona and budapest (the .txt grids and .mask.txt masks) are made
from OpenStreetMap data.

Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database
License (ODbL) 1.0:
  https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
  https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/

The extracts they were made from, the method and every hand edit are offered
under the ODbL 1.0 in tools/city in the game's source repository:
  https://github.com/alidin000/Bomberman/tree/main/tools/city

romeControl is not OSM data: it is a shipped village arena, mirrored.

The Széchenyi Chain Bridge in Budapest was designed by William Tierney Clark,
and the Hungarian Parliament Building by Imre Steindl.
