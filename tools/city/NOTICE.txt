OpenStreetMap data in tools/city

Each city folder here (rome, barcelona, budapest) holds an extract of
OpenStreetMap data, osm.json, and files made from it: raw.txt, the grids in
the budapest folder and overlay.png. The city grids and masks in
public/maps/city are made from the same extracts.

Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database
License (ODbL) 1.0:
  https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
  https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/

The extracts and the corrections made to them are offered under the ODbL 1.0.
Each meta.json records the Overpass query, its box and timestamp_osm_base;
each method.md records the method and every hand edit; tools/city/prototype
holds the code that turns an extract into a grid. The code itself is under
the repository's MIT licence.

The Széchenyi Chain Bridge in Budapest was designed by William Tierney Clark,
and the Hungarian Parliament Building by Imre Steindl. The game draws its
landmarks as stylised massing built in code.
